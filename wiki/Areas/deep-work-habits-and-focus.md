# Deep Work Habits and Focus

## Deep Work Habits

Based on Cal Newport's framework for high-value cognitive output:

- **Uninterrupted Focus Blocks**: Aim for 90-minute sessions of deep concentration.
- **Batch Shallow Tasks**: Group low-cognitive-load activities like email and messaging together.
- **Evening Shutdown**: Completely disconnect in the evening to allow for neural recovery.
