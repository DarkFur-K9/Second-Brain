# Deep Work Habits

## Deep Work Habits

Based on Cal Newport's framework for high-value cognitive output:

- **Uninterrupted Focus**: Utilize 90-minute blocks for deep work.
- **Task Batching**: Strictly batch shallow tasks such as email and messaging.
- **Evening Shutdown**: Completely shut down in the evening to allow for neural recovery.
