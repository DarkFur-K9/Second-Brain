# Vis.js Knowledge Graph Layouts

## Knowledge Graphs with Vis.js

### Layout Mechanics
- **Force-Directed Layouts**: Simulate physical forces to position nodes.
  - **Spring Tension**: Applied to edges.
  - **Coulomb Repulsion**: Applied to nodes.
- **Algorithms**: Utilizes methods such as Barnes-Hut or ForceAtlas2.

### Visual Encoding
- **Node Size**: Reflects degree centrality (number of connected notes).
- **Node Color**: Reflects thematic clusters or categories.
